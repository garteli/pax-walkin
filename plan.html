Sheet 5

Cutting and stacking the second level

Cutting

  1. Buy the 201 cm frames. Before cutting, measure the dowel and cam-lock holes at the top end of each side panel and note their distance from the top edge.
  2. Cut 53 cm off the top end of both side panels, so the finished panel is 148 cm. Keep the bottom end intact. A carpentry shop panel saw gives a chip-free cut; a hand saw will chip the melamine.
  3. Mark the hole positions you noted from the new top edge and drill them. Use the offcut as a template so the top panel fits exactly as before.
  4. Cut the thin back panel to the same length. Iron white edge banding onto the cut edges.

Stacking

  1. Build and anchor all seven ground frames first, screwed to each other side by side.
  2. A 148 × 58 frame cannot be tipped upright on top of the base, because its diagonal is 159 cm and there is only 153 cm of headroom. Assemble each upper frame in place, standing on a stepladder, or have two people lift a pre-built frame straight up above 236 cm.
  3. Screw every upper frame down through its bottom panel into the top panel of the frame below, four screws per frame, and fix each upper frame to the wall with the PAX wall brackets. D2 stands in front of the shut back leaf of the attic door, so anchor it to the wall beside the door, not through it.
  4. Keep the second level for light things: luggage, bedding, boxes. IKEA does not rate PAX for stacking, so the upper row carries little weight and relies on the wall anchors.

Where to buy

  • Hardware kit plus a made-to-length ladder. Rolling-hook kits with rails, hooks and wheels are sold by Library Ladders International and as cheaper kits on Amazon, including 3.5 m hardware sets such as the DIYHD kit. Kits usually come without the ladder or with a 2 m one; a carpentry shop makes the 392 cm ladder from the kit's drawings.
  • Israeli suppliers. Ahim Bitter sells a library ladder kit with a 2 m ladder, and Ofek Agencies imports Italscale rail ladders to the EN-131 standard. Ask both for a rail ladder cut to 392 cm with a 77° working angle.
  • Fallback without rails. An industrial rolling platform ladder with five steps and a handrail, platform at about 125 cm, reaches 384 cm and rolls on casters that lock under load. It never folds, but it is about 55 by 90 cm on the floor and has to park by the door.

Budget roughly ₪1,500 to ₪3,500 for rails, hardware and a made-to-length ladder. Not included in the totals on Sheet 4.

  • Why two drivers: a 30 W driver takes at most 10 lights and 30 W, so 14 strips at 3.5 W need two. The split also keeps every cord under 3 m, within the 3.5 m they come with. Both drivers sit just under their limits, so a 15th strip would need a third driver.

Open points

Before ordering

  • Attic door: the top edge of the leaves must be below about 375 cm for the ladder rail. The sill at 202 is in the plan. Confirm you are happy with a single 41.5 cm leaf as the opening.
  • Door swing at the entrance: which side it hinges on and whether it clears a 58 cm frame at C or a 35 cm frame at F. If it is in the way, shorten that run by one 50 cm frame.
  • The socket is on the left of the entrance wall, as you said; the 5 m lead starts there. With C and F now flush against the entrance wall, check that the entrance door's architrave does not reach into the first 58 cm on the left or the first 35 cm on the right of that wall.
  • Ceiling height at all four corners. 389 cm was measured once; old ceilings vary by a few centimetres, and the cut height follows the lowest point.
  • Eli and Mezi sections are the same size, so if one of you needs more hanging or more shoes, swap the labels rather than the frames.
  • Check the drawer, shoe shelf and mirror door prices in the IKEA Israel app. Those lines are estimates.
