The wall switches hide or show each run of frames, and attic doors, mirror doors and library ladder have their own switches. Wall and dollhouse views preset them so nothing stands in front of the frames, and you can override any of them. The spread-out view unfolds the two side walls flat beside the back wall, so every frame is seen front-on at once; the walls hinge at the back corners. Click a drawer, basket, the tray or the trouser hanger and it slides out; click again to close it. Clicking anything shows its product details on the side. The Measure button turns clicks into a tape measure: two clicks on any surfaces give the distance, with height, across-room and along-room parts in the panel.
